A Quality Standard for Rust Training

The Rust Foundation Trusted Training program (RFTT) recognizes organizations whose Rust training meets our bar for technical accuracy, instructor quality, and learner experience.

Jump to:
  • Rust Learners: View RFTT Providers
  • Rust Training Providers: Get Accredited

What Is Rust Foundation Trusted Training?

  • A Credible, Foundation-Backed Signal

    Accreditation demonstrates to the open source ecosystem that a training program meets a documented standard established by a trustworthy nonprofit foundation.

  • Built With Trainers and Members

    Process and criteria were developed with a steering committee of active Rust training providers and Rust Foundation members. A quality standard for Rust training, shaped by the organizations who’ve been in this space for years.

  • Reviewed By Experts

    A committee of active Rust training providers assesses every application independently against published criteria. We work with what you’re comfortable sharing — applicants are never required to submit sensitive materials.

  • Trustworthy Assessments

    Every application is scored against the same documented standard, applied consistently to all applicants.

What RFTT Partners Receive

The credential itself is the core benefit of RFTT, but accredited providers also gain visibility, marketing support, and business development opportunities through the Foundation.

  • Official Designation

    Market your organization as a Rust Foundation Trusted Training Provider. Display marks in association with your offerings to signal trust.

  • Visibility of Services

    Rust training offerings publicly listed on the Rust Foundation website, so developers and organizations can find and evaluate your training.

  • Amplification

    Dedicated spotlight upon announcement, plus up to one social media repost per month across Foundation channels on request.

  • Referral Opportunities

    When organizations contact the Foundation seeking training, accredited providers are the ones we point them to.

  • Exclusive Event Benefits

    15% RustConf sponsorship discount, priority consideration for conference workshops, and inclusion in the post-event digital gift bag.

  • Coordinated Sales

    Opportunities to participate in special coordinated sales promotions (e.g. Cyber Week).

Requirements

Applicants must be Rust Foundation members in good standing*, with at least two years in business and one year offering Rust training. Applications are reviewed across five areas:

  • Credibility & Ethical Practices

    A demonstrable training track record, clear business practices, and alignment with the Rust Foundation’s Code of Conduct.

  • Content Quality

    Accurate, up-to-date curriculum with clear learning outcomes, appropriate depth, and a regular update process.

  • Instructor Competence

    Verifiable Rust expertise and relevant teaching experience for all instructors delivering accredited courses.

  • Transparency & Accessibility

    Clear public-facing information on course content, structure, prerequisites, pricing, and cancellation policies. Consideration of diverse learners, delivery formats, and geographic and language coverage is a plus.

  • Evaluation & Feedback

    Mechanisms for assessing learner progress and collecting feedback to support continuous improvement of the program.

RFTT Fees

RFTT accreditation is generally only available to member organizations of the Rust Foundation offering Rust training*. All fees are separate from existing Rust Foundation membership dues, which continue on their normal billing schedule.

* If you are an individual trainer, a micro-business (up to 3 employees and less than $100,000 USD in annual revenue), and/or a non-member organization interested in pursuing RFTT accreditation, please email us at training@rustfoundation.org to explore potential options.

About the Process

The path to RFTT accreditation takes several weeks from submission to decision. Here’s what to expect at each stage. Click any step to expand and learn more.

1. Request Application

Fill out the initial interest form below.

2. Apply

The Rust Foundation verifies your eligibility. If eligible, you will receive the application form and instructions for submitting all required materials for review.

You will then submit the required materials through the application form and pay the initial application fee.

3. Independent Review

Foundation staff and the steering committee (a group of active Rust training providers committed to unbiased review) score your application.

4. Decision & Agreement

You’ll be informed of the outcome with any relevant feedback. If approved, we send the accreditation agreement and issue an invoice for the annual fee.

5. Accreditation Issued

Once the agreement is signed and the annual fee received, your two-year accreditation period starts, and benefit fulfillment begins, starting with a dedicated announcement.

Best Practices

Before starting the application form, take time to gather all required materials - it’s easy to underestimate. This will help you complete the form efficiently and avoid delays during review.

  • Prepare materials in advance
    The application form auto-saves responses, allowing you to leave and revisit later, but having everything ready will make it much easier to complete in one sitting.
  • Prepare translations if needed
    As committee members from multiple countries will review your application, all materials need to be submitted in English. Please include translations for any materials that are not in English.
  • Anonymize or redact confidential information
    Remove or obscure any sensitive client or learner information that you are not permitted to share with third parties.
  • Provide as much detail as possible
    Detailed responses help avoid follow-up questions and speed up the review process.
  • Refrain from AI-generated content
    We discourage low-effort AI-generated form responses and training materials.
  • Ask questions before submitting
    If anything is unclear or doesn’t apply to your organization, reach out to training@rustfoundation.org in advance. We’re happy to provide guidance.
  • Pay the $500 application fee by credit card
    Credit card payment (via Stripe) ensures the fastest processing. Bank transfer is available as an alternative payment option, but it may delay your application. Reviews only begin after full payment is received.

Application Checklist

Use this checklist to gather all required information and materials before starting the form.

Note: Materials marked with an asterisk (*) will be evaluated by Rust Foundation staff and the review committee. Materials without an asterisk will be evaluated by Rust Foundation staff only and will not be shared with the review committee.

1. Applicant Information

  • Organization name and DBA (if applicable)*
  • Any subsidiaries seeking accreditation (if applicable)*
  • Main contact details (first name, last name, email)
  • Organization website*
  • Country of registration
  • Organization’s legal address
  • Years in business
  • Years of offering Rust training
  • Approximate number of learners trained per year
  • Types of training programs offered*

2. Training Program Overview

  • High-level description of Rust training offerings*
  • Content update process and frequency*
  • Approach to evaluating learner understanding and learner success*
  • Public-facing marketing materials (URLs or document upload)*
  • Cancellation and refund policy
  • Methods for collecting and evaluating learner feedback*
  • If using learner surveys: Submit sample survey (file upload)*
  • At least two (2) written testimonials from clients or learners, including their names and companies*
  • Approximate number of training products (courses)*
  • If multiple courses: Full list of all Rust training programs (document upload)*

3. Training Product Information

Applicants offering more than one training product (e.g., multiple different courses) will be asked to share detailed information about at least two (2) sample products. For each submitted training product:

  • Course title*
  • Detailed course description and outline of covered topics (file upload)*
  • Training structure (progression of topics, teaching methods)*
  • Target audience*
  • Learning outcomes/goals*
  • Prerequisites (prior knowledge/skills and/or equipment needed)*
  • Details on hands-on components, if applicable (e.g., coding exercises, projects)*
  • Details on assessments, if applicable (e.g., quizzes, labs, exams)*
  • Sample course materials shared with learners (slides, readings, exercises, etc. - file upload)*

4. Instructor Information (for each sample product)

  • Full professional biography/curriculum vitae (CV) (file upload)*
  • Instructor’s technical certifications and credentials, if available*
  • Description of instructor’s teaching experience and didactic skills*
  • Any other relevant Rust experience or contributions*

5. Acknowledgements

You will need to confirm that:

  • The submitted information is accurate
  • Additional information or an interview may be required if the submitted materials are deemed insufficient by the review committee
  • Your organization agrees to report any major changes to its business model or training offerings
  • Your organization’s training program does not infringe on any third-party intellectual property
  • Your organization will abide by the Rust Foundation Code of Conduct
  • The Rust Foundation may collect feedback from your clients/learners in the future

6. Fees & Payment

Confirm preferred payment option for annual accreditation fee:

  • Pay $3,000 in full once application is approved
  • Pay a partial prorated amount once application is approved, and the rest will be added to your annual Rust Foundation membership invoice.

Confirm preferred payment option for $500 USD application fee:

  • Pay by credit card (via Stripe) immediately
  • Pay by bank transfer (provide billing contact for invoice)
Application Criteria

↓ View as PDF

All training programs submitted for accreditation must meet the following criteria:

1. Eligibility

  • Rust Foundation member organizations or self-employed/independent individuals who are providing Rust training are eligible to apply.
  • Participant must have paid the initial application fee.
  • Participant must be in business for at least 2 years and offer Rust training for at least 1 year.

2. Credibility and Ethics

  • Participant must demonstrate professional conduct and credibility.
  • Participant must disclose any major changes in business model or training products to the Rust Foundation immediately.
  • Participant’s training products may not infringe on third-party intellectual property.
  • Participant must abide by the Rust Foundation’s Code of Conduct.

3. Curriculum Quality

  • Participant demonstrates a commitment to factually correct and relevant Rust content.
  • Participant demonstrates commitment to following Rust Language best practices and documentation (including but not limited to Official Rust Language documentation, The Rust Reference, Unsafe Code Guidelines)
  • Participant demonstrates a commitment to updating content regularly.

4. Instructional Design

  • Participant’s training products must have clear learning objectives and measurable outcomes.
  • Participant’s training products must offer a logical program structure with aligning learning objectives, materials, and assessments.

5. Instructor Qualifications

  • Instructors must demonstrate proficiency in Rust that aligns with the programs they are teaching.
  • Instructors must demonstrate excellent didactic qualifications.
  • Instructors must demonstrate professionalism and credibility.

6. Transparency and Accessibility

  • Participant’s marketing materials must accurately describe the training products.
  • Participant must clearly define and communicate target audiences for each training product.
  • Participant must communicate cancellation and refund policies to learners or clients.
  • Participant must be considerate of accessibility needs.
  • Participant must demonstrate commitment to non-discrimination practices and inclusivity.

7. Evaluation and Feedback

  • Participant must have mechanisms for collecting learner feedback.
  • Participant must provide testimonials from learners or clients.
  • Participant acknowledges that the Rust Foundation reserves the right to collect direct feedback from clients/learners in the future and that this feedback must be made available to the Rust Foundation.

FAQs

What Is Rust Foundation Trusted Training?

The Rust Foundation training accreditation program establishes a trusted standard for high-quality Rust education through a consistent verification process. It supports developer growth by ensuring accuracy, consistency, and educational value across certified Rust training programs. At the same time, it helps providers build credibility and contribute to a globally accessible ecosystem of reliable Rust learning opportunities.

Who can become a Rust Foundation Trusted Training Provider?

Rust Foundation Members in good standing who offer Rust training are encouraged to apply. Individual Rust trainers and micro-businesses (three or fewer employees and annual revenue under $100,000 USD) may inquire about alternative eligibility pathways.

Organizations must be in business for at least 2 years and offer Rust training for at least 1 year.

Why should my organization become a Rust Foundation Trusted Training Provider?

Accreditation signals to both learners and clients that your training meets the Rust Foundation's standards for quality, accuracy, and professional credibility, helping you stand out from competitors. You will also gain increased visibility through directory listings, announcements, and social media amplification, along with opportunities for event participation and co-marketing campaigns. 

What are the costs for becoming a Rust Foundation Trusted Training Provider?

An initial non-refundable application fee of $500 USD is due upon submitting an application to cover the initial review costs. If the application is approved, an annual accreditation fee of $3,000 USD is due. The application fee and accreditation fee are in addition to the annual Rust Foundation membership fees. Individuals and very small businesses (with up to 3 employees and less than $100,000 USD in annual revenue) may contact training@rustfoundation.org to discuss fee structures for their specific cases. 

What criteria do Rust Foundation Trusted Training Providers have to meet?

The Rust Foundation and a steering committee composed of active Rust trainers have developed a set of criteria to ensure consistency across accredited providers. Applicants must demonstrate their commitment to credibility and ethical business practices, high-quality content, competent instructors, transparency, and accessibility. See Eligibility & Criteria for details. 

Who reviews and approves applications?

Applications are reviewed by Rust Foundation staff and a committee comprised of member representatives who are active providers of Rust training. Rust Foundation staff and committee members will independently review and score each application, then provide a recommendation on whether it should be approved or declined. If the Rust Foundation’s and steering committee’s recommendations don’t align, the Rust Foundation Board of Directors will make the final decision.

What does the application process look like?

The application process takes several weeks and consists of the following stages:

  1. The applicant fills out the interest form on the website.
  2. The Rust Foundation verifies the applicant’s eligibility. If eligible, the Rust Foundation sends the application form and instructions to collect all necessary materials for review. 
  3. The applicant submits materials for review via the application form and pays the initial application fee. 
  4. The Rust Foundation and the steering committee review and score the application.
  5. The applicant may be asked to submit additional information or materials or meet with Rust Foundation staff and the steering committee for an interview if needed to make a final decision.
  6. The applicant is informed of the final decision. If the application is approved, the Rust Foundation sends the applicant the accreditation agreement for signature and issues an invoice for the annual accreditation fees. 
  7. Once the agreement is signed by all parties and the fees have been paid, the accreditation becomes valid, and the Rust Foundation begins benefit fulfillment.
How long is the accreditation valid for?

Once approved, accreditation is valid for two years, and typically begins on the day the full fee payment has been received. After two years, the accredited provider can reapply for another two years. No application fee will be charged for renewals. 

Who should I contact with questions or requests?

Please email training@rustfoundation.org.

Is RFTT a certification for individual developers?

No, it accredits providers' general offerings and standards, not individuals or courses at this time.

Can non-English providers apply?

Yes; Materials can be submitted with English translations for review.

What happens if a provider isn't accredited?

Typically, we will provide feedback and a path to requalify.

Other Questions?

If you are a learner or an organization with other questions about the Rust Foundation Trusted Training Program, feel free to email us at training@rustfoundation.org.
